# Ledgerly Bank — Credit Card Pricing and Fee Schedule

> Ledgerly Bank is a **fictional** bank created for this demo.

## Interest and grace period
If you pay your **statement balance in full** by the due date, you pay **no interest** on new purchases (the grace period). If you pay less than the full statement balance, interest is charged on the unpaid balance carried into the next cycle, and you lose the grace period on new purchases until you pay in full again.

Interest in this demo is calculated as: **unpaid balance carried over × APR ÷ 12**, rounded to the cent. (Real cards use an average daily balance; this is simplified for transparency.)

## Purchase APR
- Everyday Card: 24.99%
- Rewards Card: 22.99%
- Promotional APR: 0% for the stated promo period, then the card's standard purchase APR applies.

## Cash advances
- Cash advance fee: **5% of the amount, minimum $10**.
- Cash advance APR: **29.99%**, charged from the day of the advance. There is **no grace period** on cash advances, so interest applies even if you pay your statement in full.
- In this demo, cash-advance interest for the cycle is calculated as: **advance amount × 29.99% ÷ 12**.

## Late payment fee
**$35** if the minimum payment is not received by the due date.

## Returned payment fee
**$35** if a payment is returned unpaid (for example, insufficient funds). The returned payment is added back to the balance.

## Foreign transaction fee
**3%** of each purchase made in a foreign currency or processed outside the US. Everyday Card only; the Rewards Card has no foreign transaction fee.

## Annual fee
- Everyday Card: $0
- Rewards Card: **$95**, charged once a year in the cardholder's account-anniversary month.

## Disputes
If you see a charge you don't recognise or were charged twice, you can **dispute it**. A specialist reviews the dispute; the charge may be temporarily credited while it is investigated. The assistant cannot reverse charges itself.
